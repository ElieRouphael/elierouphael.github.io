Research

Formal Verification of Hybrid Automata in Safety-Critical Embedded Systems
We present a framework for model-checking hybrid automata derived from real-time control loops, enabling exhaustive reachability analysis under bounded time horizons. The approach is validated on a series of automotive brake-control benchmarks.

IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control
Event-Triggered Control for Networked Autonomous Vehicles with Bounded Latency
This paper proposes an event-triggered strategy that reduces communication overhead in vehicle platoons while preserving string stability guarantees. Theoretical bounds on inter-event times are derived and confirmed through hardware-in-the-loop testing.

IEEE CDC
Real-Time Implementation of Model Predictive Control on FPGA-Based Platforms
We describe a complete pipeline for synthesising MPC controllers directly onto reconfigurable logic, achieving sub-millisecond solve times for constrained linear quadratic problems at modest resource cost. Benchmarks compare FPGA, DSP, and MCU targets.

Control Engineering Practice
Sequential Logic Synthesis for Programmable Logic Controllers: A Petri Net Approach
A structured methodology translating Grafcet specifications into verified PLC ladder programs via coloured Petri net intermediaries. Case studies from industrial conveyor and packaging lines demonstrate correctness and reduced design time.

Lyapunov-Based Stability Analysis for Switched Linear Systems Under Arbitrary Switching
We construct common quadratic Lyapunov functions for families of switched linear systems without prior knowledge of the switching signal, using sum-of-squares relaxations. Conditions for uniform exponential stability are derived analytically.

A Survey of Co-Design Methods for Embedded Control Systems
Comprehensive review of hardware/software co-design techniques for cyber-physical systems, covering scheduling theory, real-time operating systems, and task-to-core mapping strategies. Identifies open challenges in timing predictability and worst-case execution time estimation.

Robust State Estimation for Nonlinear Systems with Intermittent Observations
Extended Kalman filtering is extended to handle stochastic packet dropouts in sensor networks. Stability of the estimator is analysed through a stochastic Lyapunov approach, and performance bounds as a function of dropout probability are established.

Design and Validation of an Obstacle Avoidance Module for Indoor Mobile Robots
A reactive obstacle-avoidance algorithm for differential-drive mobile robots combines a potential-field planner with a real-time C implementation on a Cortex-M7 microcontroller. Experimental results in cluttered indoor environments demonstrate safe navigation at 0.8 m/s with sub-20 ms response latency.
