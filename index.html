Elie Rouphael

Postdoctoral Researcher · Automatic Control · System Identification

Hello, and welcome. I am Elie Rouphael, a researcher in automatic control and system identification, with a background that connects control theory, dynamic systems, estimation, and data-driven modeling. My work lies at the intersection of mathematical rigor and real-world applications, with a particular interest in building models and algorithms that help us better understand, estimate, and control complex physical systems.

I hold a PhD in automatic control and computer science, where my research focused on realization theory, stochastic and switched systems, and system identification. Alongside my research, I have taught a wide range of subjects including control theory, Python and C programming, sequential logic, embedded systems, and automation. Teaching is an important part of my work, and I care deeply about making technical ideas clear, structured, and approachable.

This website brings together the different sides of my work and interests. Here, you will find information about my research, teaching, projects, and academic activities, along with a selection of books that have inspired me and some of my photography. Beyond academia, I enjoy chess for its depth and discipline, and photography for the different way it teaches us to observe the world.
